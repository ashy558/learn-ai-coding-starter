# Sloperator Slogans

Ten ways to sell a SlopLang interpreter.

1. **"Sloperator: vibe first, then run."**
   Slap a `vibe`, ship some slop, and let the tree-walker sort out the rest.

2. **"Loadbearing code, unloadbearing syntax."**
   Your constants stay put; everything else gets to slop around.

3. **"Write a loop, make a ralph."**
   Forget `for`, forget `break`, just `ralph` until the condition says `❌`.

4. **"Hallucinate the output. Sparkle the statement."**
   Every print is a hallucination, every line ends in `✨`.

5. **"No truthiness. No parentheses. No clap emojis required."**
   Conditions are `✅` or `❌`, and the interpreter takes them literally.

6. **"`--check` first, slop later."**
   Parse the whole file before a single `vibe` runs, and get one line of error instead of a traceback.

7. **"Smart quotes or bust."**
   Straight quotes are a lexical error, so your code already looks like it came out of a chat window.

8. **"`nah` is the only else you'll ever need."**
   No `else if`, no `elif`, no `elsif` — just nest a `vibe` and let it fall through the `nah`.

9. **"Numbers as big as your ambition."**
   Arbitrary-precision integers, but no floats — nothing here can round your dreams down.

10. **"A `clanker` you can pass around."**
    Functions are first-class values, so `slop f = add✨` and call it like you mean it.
